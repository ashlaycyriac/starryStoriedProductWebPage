Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2026

Starry Stories ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and families who use our application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Starry Stories mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Device Information

We collect a unique device identifier (Vendor ID on iOS) solely for the purpose of:

  • Managing your subscription status
  • Tracking your free trial period
  • Enforcing daily story limits during the trial period

Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data including:

  • Number of stories played per day
  • Language preferences
  • App crash reports (if any)

What We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal names of children
  • Email addresses (unless you contact support)
  • Location data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Contact lists
  • Any personally identifiable information from children

2. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide and maintain our service
  • Manage subscriptions and free trials
  • Improve our app and user experience
  • Send push notifications about story completion (only if enabled)
  • Respond to customer support requests

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers hosted on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) in India. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure database storage
  • Regular security audits

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store: For subscription management and payment processing
  • Google Cloud Platform: For hosting our backend services
  • Google Gemini AI: For generating story content
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech: For audio narration
  • Expo Push Notifications: For optional story-ready notifications

We do not share any personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Starry Stories is designed for families and children. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do not require registration or login with personal details
  • Our content is reviewed to be child-appropriate
  • We do not display targeted advertising

6. Subscriptions and Payments

All payments are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID. Subscription management is handled entirely by Apple.

7. Data Retention

We retain your device information and usage data only as long as necessary to provide our services. If you delete the app and do not use it for 12 months, we may delete your associated data from our servers.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about the data we hold about your device
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of push notifications through device settings

To exercise these rights, please contact us at ashlaycyriac@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: ashlaycyriac@gmail.com
