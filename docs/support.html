Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

New users get a 5-day free trial with up to 5 stories per day. After the trial, you can subscribe to Premium for unlimited access.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel anytime through your App Store subscription settings. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.

What languages are supported?

We support 12 languages including English, Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Arabic.

Is the content safe for children?

Yes absolutely! We use advanced AI with strict safety filters to ensure all stories are age-appropriate and family-friendly.

🔐 Privacy & Data

We take your privacy seriously. Starry Stories does not collect personal information from children. We only use device identifiers for subscription management.

💳 Subscription & Billing

Subscriptions are billed through Apple's App Store. Prices are in Indian Rupees (INR). Subscription automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before.

🛡️ Child Safety

All content is AI-generated with safety filters. We are committed to providing a safe, educational, and entertaining experience for children of all ages.

📱 Technical Support

If you're experiencing technical issues, please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the problem when contacting support.
